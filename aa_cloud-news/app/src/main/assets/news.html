Configs

Appearance

Dark is the default. System follows your device's light/dark setting and updates live.

Source

Pick where articles come from. Only the self-hosted GDELT source provides tone/sentiment and timeline data — public RSS feeds carry headlines only.

Reading from a publisher's own feed (Google, BBC, etc.) means that publisher sees your device's IP address and the fact that you're interested in that feed — the self-hosted GDELT source does not have this effect.

Topics

The GDELT queries this app tracks. Only query-driven sources (GDELT, Google News search) use these — a fixed feed like BBC or Al Jazeera ignores this list.

Demo data

Auto shows demo data only while your real topics/articles are empty, and switches itself to real data the moment any land. On always shows demo; Off never does (even if that means empty lists). Demo headlines are fabricated and deliberately generic — never real reporting.

Images

Off by default: article thumbnails load from arbitrary publisher domains, which leaks your IP to every outlet and breaks offline use. Turn on to load them lazily (each degrades gracefully on failure).

Some thumbnails may still fail to load: this app blocks insecure (http://) images outright, and not every publisher serves their images over https.
